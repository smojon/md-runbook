# Share safely

- **Export Report (HTML)**: a readable record of a run, redacted.
- **Copy for AI**: the runbook with outputs, names pseudonymized and a leak check first.
- AI-proposed cells never run until you approve them.

Settings that could weaken these can only be changed in your user settings, never by a
repository.

**Useful to you?** A [rating](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=smojon.md-runbook&ssr=false#review-details)
helps others find md-runbook, and an [issue](https://github.com/smojon/md-runbook/issues) tells us
what is missing or broken.
