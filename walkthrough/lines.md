# Line by line

The dropdown next to a cell's ▶ has:

- **Run Line or Selection** (`Ctrl+Alt+Enter`): only the line under the cursor, or the selection;
- **Run Lines One by One**: each command in turn, stopping at the first that fails;
- **Step Through Lines**: pause before each line, like a debugger (`F10` next, `F5` the rest,
  `Shift+F5` stop).

`cd` and variables carry over from one line to the next.

Try it on section 13 of the sample runbook (**md-runbook: Open Sample Runbook**).
