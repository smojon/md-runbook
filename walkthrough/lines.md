# Line by line

- **Hover a command** (cells with two or more): a plain click on **Run line** runs it, on
  **Check** checks it. **Run N checked lines** under the cell runs the checked ones. **Run
  Checked Lines** in the dropdown next to the cell's run button does the same.
- **The dropdown** also has:
  - **Run Lines One by One**: each command in turn, stopping at the first that fails;
  - **Step Through Lines**: pause before each line, like a debugger (`F10` next, `F5` continue,
    `Shift+F5` stop).
- **Select text** in a cell and **Run Selection** (dropdown, right-click, or `Ctrl+Alt+Enter`)
  runs exactly that.
- **Breakpoints:** click the margin left of a line for a red dot; running the cell then pauses
  there.

`cd` and variables carry over from one line to the next.

Try it on section 13 of the sample runbook (**md-runbook: Open Sample Runbook**).
