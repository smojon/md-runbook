# Open a runbook

A runbook is an ordinary Markdown file. **Open as Runbook** shows each fenced `bash`, `pwsh` or
`cmd` block as a cell you can run. Everything else stays Markdown.

To open one: right-click a `.md` file, or click the notebook icon in the editor title bar.

The sample opens as an untitled runbook: nothing is written until you save it. None of its cells
change your machine.
